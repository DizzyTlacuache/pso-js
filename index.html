Particle Swarm Optimization

"I'm a swarm of code. There are no strings on me."

Age of Ultron workshop: command a satellite swarm and hunt for vibranium.

A code experiment by EIC.

The mission

  • Ultron needs vibranium to complete his plan. We must find it first.
  • Vibranium emits a very specific radiation signature.
  • The closer a satellite gets, the stronger the signal it reads.
  • We control a swarm of satellites and don't know where the deposit is.
  • The tool that coordinates the swarm: Particle Swarm Optimization.

What is PSO?

  • A search method inspired by flocks of birds and schools of fish.
  • Many simple "particles" (our satellites) explore the search area at the same time.
  • Each satellite remembers the strongest signal it has read.
  • The swarm shares the strongest signal anyone has read.
  • Together, they converge on the vibranium.

How does a satellite move?

Every step, a satellite's velocity combines three influences:

  • Inertia: keep going in the current direction.
  • Cognitive: go back toward my own best position.
  • Social: go toward the swarm's best position.

velocity = inertia × velocity + c1 × r1 × (myBest − position) + c2 × r2 × (swarmBest − position)

What you will see

  • The map hides a vibranium deposit. The satellites don't know where it is.
  • They only read the radiation signal at their own position: stronger means closer.
  • The status changes to Vibranium located when the swarm gathers on it.
  • Try it: low vs. high inertia, cognitive only, social only, few vs. many satellites.
  • Press Restart to bury the vibranium somewhere new.
